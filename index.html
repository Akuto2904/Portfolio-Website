About Me

I am a BSc Computer Science student at the University of Dundee, driven by a passion for full-stack software engineering and AI model efficiency

My technical journey is focused on applying problem-solving and system optimization skills to scalable, real-world products. I have built a strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented programming using C++, Java, and Python.
